# PLAYCE 1.1.0 (7)

App Store "What's New" copy. Paste each locale into App Store Connect. Paredón is not mentioned: it is not part of this release.

## English

- New 21 training mode on iPhone and Apple Watch.
- An in-progress match is restored when you reopen the app (score, names, and server).
- Improvements to the Apple Watch scoreboard and to sharing a finished match.

## Español

- Nuevo modo de entrenamiento 21 en iPhone y Apple Watch.
- Si dejás un partido a medias, se recupera al volver a abrir la app (marcador, nombres y saque).
- Mejoras en el marcador del Apple Watch y al compartir un partido terminado.
